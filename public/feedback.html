Suggest an improvement, or report a problem

Feedback becomes a public issue on RangerTrak’s GitHub repository. That’s the standard for this open-source project, and it’s stated here up front, before you submit anything.

Noting something during an incident? Use the AAR note (after-action review) button in the app instead. It stays private on the device until you choose to send it.

Do not enter confidential information: names, phone numbers, addresses or anything from a real mission.

Optional. Left blank, nobody can follow up with you directly.

Support RangerTrak

RangerTrak is free and will stay free. If it's useful to your team and you'd like to help keep it going, you can buy the developer a coffee. That goes to John Cornelison personally, not a charity, so it isn't tax-deductible. Honest feedback helps just as much.
